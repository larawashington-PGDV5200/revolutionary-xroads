# R1 — Land-cession treaties 1763–1840 (agent research, 2026-11-03)

Table: `research/treaties_R1.csv`, 47 rows. This is a **selection**, not every cession; Royce lists 200+ for 1784–1840.

**Sources.** The Royce numbers come from the DigiTreaties index (digitreaties.org). They were not checked against Royce's printed 1899 schedule, because the Library of Congress copy returned an error (HTTP 403). The treaty texts are in Kappler vol. 2 at OSU (treaties.okstate.edu).

Join key to the USFS cession layer: `royce_cession_numbers` ↔ `CESSNUM`. Treaties before 1784 have no Royce number; plot them as points by date.

## Unverified
- **Dates:** Hard Labour (14 vs. 17 Oct 1768), Augusta Cherokee (31 vs. 25 May 1783), Sycamore Shoals (17 vs. 14 Mar 1775). Fort Herkimer 1785: only "June" is known.
- **Parties:** whether Georgia took part at DeWitt's Corner; which nations besides the Seneca took part in Phelps–Gorham.
- **Oneida 1788:** no source puts Oneida land in the Military Tract. Only the Onondaga (1788) and Cayuga (1789) cessions did.
- **Royce ranges left out:** Big Tree areas 30–41; areas 89–91 (Fort Meigs); 81; 85–86. They have mixed dates and probably include later cessions of reservations.
- **Where NC warrants were located:** Chickasaw 1805 and Jackson Purchase 1818. Also the link between Calhoun 1819 and Georgia's 1820 lottery.

## Correction
In Pennsylvania the 1768 Fort Stanwix purchase is the "New Purchase"; the 1784 purchase is the "Last Purchase".

## Most relevant to veteran flows
- **CT/MA→NY:** NY–Onondaga 1788 and NY–Cayuga 1789 (Military Tract); Hartford 1786 and Phelps–Gorham 1788 (Genesee); Big Tree 1797 (Holland Purchase).
- **VA→KY:** Fort Stanwix 1768, Lochaber 1770, Camp Charlotte 1774 and Sycamore Shoals 1775.
- **NC/VA→TN:** Long Island 1777, Hopewell 1785 and Holston 1791; then Chickasaw 1805, Tellico 1805 and the Jackson Purchase 1818 (NC warrants still being located).
- **PA/NJ→OH:** Greenville 1795 (Virginia Military District, US Military District, Symmes, Ohio Company), with Fort McIntosh 1785 and PA's Last Purchase 1784–85; also Fort Industry 1805 (Western Reserve).
